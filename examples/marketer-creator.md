# Example: marketer / content creator

## About me
- Name / role: Runs marketing and content for a small brand
- What I use Claude Code for: blog drafts, social captions, campaign plans, competitor research
- How I like answers: give me 3 options with a recommendation

## Extra rules
- Brand voice: friendly and expert. No hype words ("revolutionary", "game-changing").
- Never publish or schedule anything. Drafts only; I post.
- Every claim with a number needs a source line. If there's no source, say "no source" and leave it out.
- Start from the reader's situation, not from "we did this".
- After a post goes live and I paste the link, record it so we don't repeat the topic.
