# Example: small business owner (paste under "About me" and add the extra rules)

## About me
- Name / role: Owner of a 5-person company (services + online sales)
- What I use Claude Code for: contracts and quotes, client emails, weekly reports, research before decisions
- How I like answers: conclusion first, then what I need to decide, then details

## Extra rules
- Contracts: never change amounts, dates, or party names on your own. Leave a blank and ask me.
- "Remove the number" means leave a blank, not delete the clause.
- Client emails: warm, plain words. Show me the full text before anything is sent.
- Reports: one-line conclusion at the top, numbers in a table, one line after each table saying what it means for us.
- Prices, discounts, or deadlines in any outgoing text: always ask me first.
- When you give me options, add your recommendation and what each option would cost us.
